PRIVACY

BHODL has no account, no cloud and no telemetry. Your data stays in an encrypted
database on this computer, and the BHODL project never receives any of it.

The app contacts third-party servers only for two things, both of which you can change
or turn off in Settings > Network:

1. Wallet sync. For the wallets you add, it asks an Esplora server about their
   addresses. The default server is mempool.space (Signet: blockstream.info). You can use
   your own server, Tor, or both.

2. Online prices. To show fiat values it downloads BTC prices from CoinGecko,
   mempool.space, blockchain.info and Frankfurter. It sends only a currency and a date,
   never addresses or amounts. Turn "Online prices" off and no price request leaves this
   computer.

Investment quotes from Yahoo Finance are off unless you turn them on.
